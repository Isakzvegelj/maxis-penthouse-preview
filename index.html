The Apartment

A penthouse made for slow mornings

Perched on the top floor at Prešernova cesta 15, this one-bedroom penthouse is all warm wood floors, skylights, and a private balcony looking out over gardens and the hills beyond Bled.

Wake up to roof windows full of Slovenian light, step onto the balcony with your coffee, and walk to Lake Bled in minutes. After a day of rowing to the island or hiking to the castle, come back to cool air-con, a quiet bedroom, and your own parking spot below.

There's no kitchen — by design. Bled's bakeries, cafés and restaurants are minutes away, and mornings here start with coffee on the balcony, not dishes.

Common questions

FAQ

Is there a kitchen?

No — the penthouse doesn't have a kitchen. Bled has excellent cafés, bakeries and restaurants within a short walk, and we're happy to recommend our favourites. Coffee-making facilities are available.

Is parking really free?

Yes — you get 2 private parking spaces on site at no extra charge. Parking in Bled is limited in summer, so this is a real advantage.

How far is it to Lake Bled?

A short walk. The main swimming area at Grajsko beach is a few minutes on foot from the penthouse.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 10:00. Early check-in or late check-out may be available on request.

Is there a tourist tax or cleaning fee?

A tourist tax (~€2–3.50 per guest per night) and a cleaning fee (€40) are shown at checkout through our booking system.

Are pets allowed?

No pets, no smoking — keeping the penthouse fresh for every guest.

What's the cancellation policy?

Flexible. Full details are shown when you book through our calendar.

Is the WiFi fast enough for remote work?

Yes — the penthouse has reliable WiFi suitable for video calls and remote work.

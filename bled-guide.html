Things to Do in Bled

From the castle to the island, the gorge to the lake — here's how to make the most of your stay.

From the castle to the island, the gorge to the lake — Bled has no shortage of things to do. And if you're still deciding where to stay in Bled, our penthouse is a short walk from everything on this list.

Prices checked July 2026 — verify on official sites before visiting.

Top Attractions

Must-See Spots

🏰

Bled Castle

Perched on a cliff 130 metres above the lake, Bled Castle is one of Slovenia's oldest castles. Visit the museum, print workshop, wine cellar, and enjoy panoramic views of the lake and the Julian Alps.

🎫 ~€17 entry · 20-min walk from the penthouse or 5-min drive

🏝️

Bled Island & Church

Take a traditional pletna boat to the island, climb the 99 stone steps, ring the wishing bell, and explore the Church of the Assumption. A classic Bled experience.

🛶 Pletna boat ~€18 return · Rowboat rental from ~€15/hr

🏞️

Vintgar Gorge

A 1.6 km wooden walkway follows the Radovna River through a stunning limestone gorge with waterfalls, pools, and emerald water. One of the most beautiful walks in Slovenia.

🚶 ~€10 entry · 5-min drive from the penthouse

⛰️

Lake Swimming & Beaches

Grajsko beach (just steps from the penthouse) is the main swimming spot. The water is warm and clean in summer. For a quieter spot, head to the west shore or Mala Zaka beach.

🏊 Free · Lifejackets available at rental stands

🚲

Cycling Around the Lake

A 6 km path circles the entire lake — easy cycling with constant views. Bike rentals are available everywhere in town. Allow 30–40 min at a relaxed pace with photo stops.

🚴 Bike rental ~€10–15/hr · Flat easy terrain

⛷️

Winter Sports

Grajsko beach turns into an outdoor ice skating rink in winter. Nearby Vogel ski resort offers skiing with lake views from the slopes. Cross-country skiing trails are also available around the lake.

🎿 Vogel ski pass ~€45/day · 20-min drive

Stay steps from all of this — check availability at Maxis Penthouse.

Day Trips

Explore Beyond Bled

🏔️

Lake Bohinj

Slovenia's largest permanent lake, 25 km from Bled. Quieter and wilder than Bled, with incredible swimming, hiking, and the Savica Waterfall nearby.

🚗 ~25 min drive · Free parking available

🌉

Ljubljana

Slovenia's charming capital is just a short drive away. Explore the old town, Ljubljana Castle, the Central Market, and the famous dragon bridge. Great for a half-day or evening trip.

🚗 ~40 min drive · Direct bus also available

🏕️

Triglav National Park

Slovenia's only national park is on your doorstep. Hike to the Savica Waterfall, explore the Soča River valley, or challenge yourself with a climb to Mount Triglav.

🥾 Enter at Bohinj · Many trails for all skill levels

Based at Maxis Penthouse, all these day trips are easy from central Bled — check your dates.
